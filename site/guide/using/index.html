Using it

The rail on the left is the whole app: six pages you read, and two you set up. Keys 1 to 8 switch between them, and the season picker at the top right looks back at earlier seasons.

Session

The app opens here. While a map is open you see its name and tier, the clock, kills, the share of the map cleared and how you stand against your best clear. Between maps it shows the last run and the session so far: the best find, the other drops, the runs and your fastest clears. Under the session's totals, your level: how far into it you are, what the session added, what deaths cost (after picking up your corpse) and, while you play, when you reach the next level at this pace. It hides once a character reaches 99.

Runs

Every map run with its tier, time, kills, kills per minute, map events and notable drops. By map compares your maps (how often you ran each, best and typical clear, a trend line); Log lists the runs one by one, with filters. Zones and Bossing cover what isn't a map.

The buttons at the top right switch between your characters, or show All of them together and compare them.

Drops

The season's haul on top: ten cards for your best drops, ranked by your item tiers. Pin any drop of the season to a card. Below it, one day at a time: pick the day on a calendar shaded by drops, or step with ← and →. Hover an item for its in-game tooltip.

Currency counts runes, keys, essences and the like per day. Tiers is where the ranking comes from: drag items between tiers, rename and recolour them, and export or import the list as a file. Cube slams and bricks are counted, and crafted items are recorded as crafts.

Grail

Every unique and set in the game, found or missing, all time or by season. First finds are marked as they drop, and milestones are kept.

Session history

Under the session, its history for the season picked at the top (or all time): your totals, then every session as a row with its day and hours, how long it lasted, its maps, zones and boss fights, kills, best find and who played. Open one to see it the way the session looked when it ended. Below the list, When you play: a calendar of kills, maps, drops or Worldstone Shards per day, hour by hour for short ranges; click a day for its sessions. A session is a run of games with no break longer than 45 minutes, across characters.

Trade

New valuable drops wait here to be listed or stashed. Listings you post on the PD2 trade site sync back; mark them sold with the price you really got, in HR. Magic, rare and crafted finds show while they sit in your stash.

Horazon shows no market prices or estimates: only the prices you enter yourself.

Keys

  • 1 to 7: Session, Runs, Drops, Grail, Trade, Stream, Setup.
  • /: jump to the page's search.
  • ← →: the day before or after, on Drops.
